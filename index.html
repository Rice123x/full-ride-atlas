How to read the tiers. “Guaranteed full ride” = funding is attached to admission itself (rare). “Full need-based aid” = you pay what a formula says your family can afford — low-income families typically pay nothing, but it is not a merit prize. “Competitive full scholarship” = a separate contest (government schemes like MEXT, GKS, CSC, Stipendium Hungaricum and Türkiye Bursları, or university awards like Pearson and Jefferson). “Tuition scholarship” = fees covered, living costs yours. Policy tiers (tuition-free / low tuition) are not scholarships at all.

Accuracy. Rankings (QS/THE/US News) are approximate 2025–26-edition bands; acceptance rates and populations are rounded; deadlines shown are typical cycles. All of it drifts — verify on the official pages linked in every card, and treat “Needs verification” labels as exactly that. Compiled July 2026.
